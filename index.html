Hello there! I'm Anjaney, currently a Performance & Risk Analyst at Thames Water, where I spend my days making sure over 1,000 waste pumping stations across London behave themselves — and gently pointing out the ones that don't. Armed with an MSc in Data Science from the University of Exeter and an unhealthy affection for spreadsheets, I turn SCADA alarms, SAP asset records, and telemetry data into decisions people can actually act on.

On paper, my job is data analysis, statistical modelling, automation, and root-cause investigation. In practice, it's a mix of detective work, translation (SCADA and SAP don't naturally speak the same language, so I ran the workshops that got them talking), and the occasional Power Automate flow that saves someone a very tedious Tuesday. Recent highlights include mapping 94% of alarms to their assets in the Loddon region — helping push performance compliance from 10% to 85% in a single year — and contributing to £2.3M in energy savings across 2025–2026.

Beyond the day-to-day, I lead sprint planning for the team, step in as interim manager when needed, mentor new joiners, and drive programmes like Wet Well Cleaning that have prevented over 1,000 pollution incidents. I'm also the person you'd call if the Power BI gateway ever falls over — mostly because I wrote the script that quietly puts it back up before anyone notices.
