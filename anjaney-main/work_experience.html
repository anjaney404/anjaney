Performance & Risk Analyst

Thames Water, United Kingdom | November 2024 – Present

As Performance & Risk Analyst at Thames Water, I lead performance analysis and risk management for over 1,000 waste pumping stations across the London region — spanning London North, London South, and Mogden. Using Python, SQL, Power BI, DAX, Power Automate, and SSMS, I work at the intersection of data engineering, operational analytics, and asset management, integrating SCADA, SAP, SAP Business Object, Optima, LMC, and iHub into a single view of asset health, risk, and compliance.

Key programmes and initiatives:

  • Asset Availability — SCADA/SAP mapping (Sewage Treatment Works): Acted as tech lead and consultant for the SCADA-to-SAP mapping workshops, aligning alarms to the assets they belong to. Successfully mapped 94% of alarms in the Loddon region as a proof of concept, which lifted performance compliance from 10% to 85% within a single year. Now being rolled out to other regions and adapted for the waste pumping station estate.
  • Asset Availability exclusions: Accountable for daily availability exclusions — identifying sites where telemetry (Available / Unavailable / Failed / Inhibited) is missing and ensuring the final availability numbers reflect a fair, defensible view of the estate.
  • Weekly Intervention Calls: Lead the intervention process for London's waste pumping stations — reviewing backlog sites, surfacing newly stressed sites from Pump Run-Time, SCADA Alarms, Stressed Site Reporting, Energy Tracker, and Pump Behaviour reports, and presenting them to regional managers. Contributed to £2.3M in energy savings across 2025–2026, an outcome acknowledged directly by the CEO.
  • Technical Support Manager (TSM) forum: Chair the monthly TSM call, gathering feedback from technical managers, tracking existing projects, and scoping new ones. Programmes delivered under this forum include the Wet Well Cleaning Programme, Asset Improvement Programme, Data Collection App, and Black Start Testing.
  • Wet Well Cleaning Programme (WWC): Designed the criticality framework that prioritises which pumping station wet wells get cleaned and how often, based on location, historical pollution incidents, and pump behaviour. Set the annual benchmark, review progress quarterly, and coordinate with the LMC contractor team. The programme has prevented over 1,000 pollution incidents.
  • Asset Improvement Programme (AIP): Identify pumping stations missing ultrasonic wet-well level sensors and/or backup control, then coordinate installation and track rollout.
  • Data pipelines, gateways, and data quality: Own the accuracy of daily data ingestion across the reporting estate. Built a monitoring flow that tracks file arrivals and alerts when expected files are late, and wrote a self-healing script for the Power BI gateway that restarts the gateway task if it drops and posts a status message in Teams — turning a silent failure into a non-event.
  • Ad-hoc analysis and internal consulting: Deliver a steady stream of historical data requests for individual pumping stations, always paired with a stakeholder walkthrough so people leave with understanding rather than just a spreadsheet.
  • Leadership and team development: Acted as interim manager on multiple occasions during extended absences, running the team of four. Inducted, trained, and mentored a new joiner to full autonomy in three months. Currently also covering the projects of a colleague on year-long maternity leave.
  • DevOps sprint planning: Self-taught DevOps sprint planning after the department introduced it, and now run sprint planning for the team while training the wider department in parallel.
